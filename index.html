Viewer mail · your questions

Questions. And honest answers.

Can another robot check the first robot?

It will just agree. They both learned from the same internet. Two robots agreeing is not a second opinion. It is an echo.

Are people slow?

Very. A person takes forty minutes to do what a robot does in four seconds. That slowness is what you are paying for. It means somebody actually read it.

Do your people use robots?

They are allowed to. Mostly they do not, because they have read what robots write. A real person signs every answer, so a real person is on the hook.

What if I do not like the answer?

That happens a lot, and it is not a refund. You can ask a second person. The second person usually agrees with the first one. Sorry.
