Viewer mail · anticipated objections

Questions we get, mostly from people who already know the answer.

Can't I just get another AI to check the first AI?

You absolutely can, and it will agree with the first one. They went to the same school, read the same internet, and share the same confident tone about things neither of them has verified. Two models agreeing is not a second opinion. It is an echo with a progress bar.

Isn't a human dramatically slower?

Yes. Enormously. A human takes forty minutes to do what a model does in four seconds, and charges you for all forty. That latency is the product. It is the sound of someone actually reading it.

Do your humans use AI to do the reviews?

They're permitted to, and mostly they don't, on the grounds that they have read the output. Every verdict is signed by a named person who is professionally accountable for it. If a review is wrong, there is a human to be wrong — which, it turns out, is the whole service.

What if I don't like the verdict?

That is the most common outcome and it is not, on its own, grounds for a refund. You may request a second human at half rate. The second human usually agrees with the first one, which we appreciate is not the news you were hoping for.

How do I know your humans are any good?

Every inspector's background, number and specialism is on the file before you commit. Send us one item for $49 and judge the review the way we judge your output: by reading it closely and deciding whether it's actually any good.
